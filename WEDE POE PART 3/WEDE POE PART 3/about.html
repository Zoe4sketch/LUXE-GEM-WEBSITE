Who we are

Style, quality and a personal touch.

Luxe Gems Jewellery is a retail jewellery business founded by Amara in 2022. The business has two branches, one in Pretoria and one in Johannesburg.

The collection includes rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings, watches and other fashion accessories. The website gives customers an easy way to discover products, learn about the branches and send enquiries before visiting a store.
